Support

Zählerwerk helps you read your meters for electricity, water, gas, heat pumps and more, and shows consumption and cost per month. Your data stays on your device.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to zaehlerwerk@gmx.de and include your device and app version (Settings, at the bottom).

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Zählerwerk has no account and sends no records to a server.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new device?

In the app, go to Settings → Backup → “Back up” and save the file, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new device, choose that file under “Restore”.

I bought Zählerwerk Pro, but it isn’t active on my new device.

Settings → Zählerwerk Pro → “Restore purchase”. The purchase works on all devices with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Google Play.
