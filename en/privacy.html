What data is processed

Zählerwerk stores meter readings, purchases, tariffs, measurement periods and your settings only on this device. The app does not send this data to the provider or to third parties. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

In-app purchase

If you buy Zählerwerk Pro, Apple or Google handles the purchase. The provider receives no payment details, only the information that the purchase exists. The privacy policy of the respective store applies.

Backups

You create backup files yourself and decide where they are stored or with whom they are shared; the terms of that storage location apply.

Retention

Your data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or remove the app or its data.

Your rights

As the provider receives no data from you, all data remains with you. For privacy questions, contact the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; the competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
